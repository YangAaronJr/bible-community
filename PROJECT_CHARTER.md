## Core Guiding Principles (Version 1.0 MVP)
1. **Grace over Guilt:** No streaks that reset to zero, no timers, no pressure metrics.
2. **True Intimacy:** Strictly invite-only circles capped at 12 members. No public feeds, global leaderboards, or follower counts.
3. **Physical-Digital Harmony:** Full compatibility with physical Bibles and paper notebooks (check off chapters read physically and upload photos of handwritten reflections).
4. **Gentle Fellowship:** Pastoral/mentor badges remain optional; encouragement nudges ("Rings") come with quiet 24-hour cooldowns.
